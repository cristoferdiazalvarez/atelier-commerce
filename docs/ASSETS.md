# Photography and image sources

Images are illustrative stock photography, downloaded to `public/images` and served locally. They are not photographs of merchandise for sale. No claim is made that materials, variants or product descriptions match the pictured garments. Colors are editorially desaturated for the neutral visual direction.

Source and license checked on 2026-11-13: [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). It permits downloading, modifying and using free images, including noncommercial use, subject to its restrictions. This repository is not an image resale service or stock image collection. No photographer identity or model endorsement is invented. Before any real commercial launch, replace illustrative assets with commissioned product photography and review likeness/trademark rights.
